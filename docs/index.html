Dates are session dates. A file fetched on the 5th carries the 4th session's settled open interest, and is shown as the 4th.
Open interest settles overnight, so this is prior-close positioning, not live. The Cboe feed is ~15 minutes delayed.
Dealers assumed long calls, short puts — the standard public convention, not a measurement of real positioning.
Walls ignore strikes within 0.4% of spot. Gamma peaks at the money, so without that exclusion the "wall" is just the ATM strike.
Support & resistance lists option concentrations inside ±1σ, where σ = spot × ATM IV30 × √(30/365), from the settled chain. Density (0–1) = 0.6×√(net gamma ÷ largest bin within ±10%) + 0.4×√(open interest ÷ largest), so the bigger wall scores near 1 and a round-number OI pile still registers. Persistence uses the Sector Monitor’s wall-strength rules over the last ten sessions: a session counts as held when a bin within one strike of the level was a top-quartile concentration with the same gamma sign; strong = held 80%+ of weighted sessions or 6+ in a row, steady = 50%+ or 3+, shifting = less, fresh = not a level last session, new = under three sessions of history. Sessions before a monthly expiration count half, before a quarterly a quarter. Grade = 50×persistence + 50×density, cut at the Sector Monitor’s 75 / 60 / 45: A strong, B solid, C moderate, D weak (no history caps at B). “d” is distance in daily moves (IV30÷16). Levels are prior-close positioning, like the walls.
Chart panels use different gamma sources — bars from Cboe's reported gamma, the profile from Black-Scholes with IV held fixed. The flip location is robust; the profile's magnitudes are not comparable to the bars.
